    डाक टिकट

    Minimal art, collected like stamps

    A side practice I post on social media: festivals, tributes and days that matter, each reduced to one idea and as few marks as it takes.

    It is the same discipline as my product work. A trishul wearing a mask, an oxygen cylinder standing in for a letter, a mother hidden inside the word for mask: each piece has to be understood in a second, in a feed, by anyone. Many were made in 2020 and 2021, when a festival greeting could also carry a public health message. Pick a stamp to read the idea behind it.

      तस्वीरें

      One word, one frame

      Street and still-life photographs from Mumbai, shot on a phone, led by the frame National Geographic featured, shot on a phone. Each carries a single word set into the scene, often letting the subject finish the word: a man on a crutch becomes the L of ALONE, two strangers become the M and N of MOBILE CONNECTION.

      It is how I look at the city: the gap between people and the systems around them, children who play anyway, and small jokes hiding in a cup of chai. The prints develop as you reach them. Pick one for the story behind it.

        ब्रज की होली

        Braj Holi

        A photo series from Holi in Braj, around Barsana and Nandgaon, where the festival lasts days and Lathmar Holi turns the streets into theatre: women swing lathis, men from Nandgaon take the blows on painted shields, and everyone ends up the same colour.

        I went to photograph it and came back soaked. These are people, not props, so I kept close and let the colour do the talking. Drag or use the arrows to walk through the series.

          गुढीपाडवा

          Gudi Padwa

          A photo series from the Gudi Padwa shobha yatra in Mumbai, the Marathi new year, when gudis rise from windows and the streets fill with nauvari sarees, phetas, dhol and flags.

          Whole families dress up together: children become gods, warriors and freedom fighters, girls swing swords and climb ropes, and grandparents watch from balconies. I went for the portraits and found a whole city playing dress-up with its own history.
